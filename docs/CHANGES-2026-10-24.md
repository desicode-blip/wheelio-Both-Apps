# Changes: staging, 23–24 October 2026 (Customer and Captain apps)

For engineers picking up the code after `9b8b661` ("Fix refund recovery and complete test web acceptance").
Everything here is on `staging`. The direction changed in this period: **the mobile apps (iOS first, then
Android) are now the focus, built from the Flutter code in `apps/flutter/`. The web build is not being
developed further for now.** The SwiftUI apps in `apps/ios/` are unchanged.

Most customer-app changes below run in the **sample-data app** only (phone builds use `WHEELIO_DEMO=true`).
Each one that the Firebase backend must support is listed in `docs/BACKEND-HOOKUP.md` with what to change.

## 1. iOS builds of the Flutter apps

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| `ios/` platform added to both apps (`flutter create --platforms=ios`, Flutter 3.38.6) | `apps/flutter/customer/ios`, `apps/flutter/captain/ios` |
| Bundle IDs `com.amanmaheshwari.wheelio.customer` / `.captain` (same as the SwiftUI apps, so an install replaces them), team `8CCQQM7TP3`, iOS 15+, portrait on iPhone, camera/photos/mic/location texts | `ios/Runner.xcodeproj/project.pbxproj`, `ios/Runner/Info.plist`, `ios/Podfile` |
| CocoaPods set up (`Podfile.lock`, Pods build phases) | `ios/` |
| Xcode's Run uses the **Release** configuration, so an app installed from Xcode keeps opening without Xcode attached | `ios/Runner.xcodeproj/xcshareddata/xcschemes/Runner.xcscheme` |
| `--dart-define=WHEELIO_DEMO=true` runs the sample-data app (the phone equivalent of the web's `?demo=true`) | `packages/washkrew_firebase/lib/src/portal.dart` (`launchWashKrew`), `live_backend.dart` (`WashKrewRuntime.demo`) |
| The yellow "DEMO · Sample data" strip is hidden on phone builds (kept on the web) | `portal.dart` (`DemoLabel`) |

Notes for running on a Mac: Apple-silicon Macs need Rosetta for Flutter's USB helper
(`sudo softwareupdate --install-rosetta --agree-to-license`). With a free Apple account, installed apps
stop opening after 7 days and need reinstalling. Live Firebase on phones is **not** wired yet (iOS Firebase
apps, native phone sign-in, native Razorpay); see "Native iPhone builds" in `BACKEND-HOOKUP.md`.

## 2. Shared design system (`packages/washkrew_ui`, both apps)

- **Footers ride above the keyboard.** `WScreen` pads by the keyboard height, so buttons like Next stay visible
  while typing. (`components.dart`, `WScreen`)
- **Bottom sheets fit their content.** `showWSheet` grows to its content up to 90% of the screen and scrolls
  beyond that; the `height` argument is now only kept for old call sites. Fixes sheets cut off at the top or
  bottom (payment sheet). Captain's sign-up nudge sheet replaced a `Spacer` with a fixed gap for this.
  (`components.dart`, `captain/lib/screens/welcome.dart`)

## 3. Customer app

### Welcome (C02)
Slides are a `PageView` carousel: picture and words move together under a finger (or mouse), the dots follow
the scroll position, and Back / Next / Skip stay fixed. Auto-advance pauses while dragging.
(`screens/start.dart`, `_WelcomeScreenState`, `_SlideDots`)

### Vehicle number (C06)
"Use the number on your plate" helper removed. A small tilted example plate (blue "IND" strip,
`TS 07 HB 2024`) with "Looks like this / Spaces or no spaces, both are fine" sits under the field.
(`screens/booking.dart`, `_PlateHint`)

### Address by hand (new C09d)
"Enter address by hand" on the address search now works. Flow: search → **Type your address** (society or
building pre-filled from the search, area, 6-digit pincode, city defaulting to Hyderabad) → Floor and slot
(asks for the tower; nothing pre-picked) → rest of the booking. Saved as a `Society` with id `manual-…`.
Sample app only; the live app still lists only served societies.
(`screens/booking.dart`, `ManualAddressScreen`; route `N.manualAddress`; gallery `C09d`)

### Time slot (C12)
Several windows can be picked, plus **Any time of the day** (selects every open window; tap again to clear).
`TimeSlot` gained `windows` and `anyTime`; touching windows read as one ("10 AM – 2 PM"); review shows e.g.
"Tomorrow, Any time (6 AM – 6 PM)". The live app keeps single-window booking until the backend supports more.
(`data/models.dart` `TimeSlot`; `screens/booking.dart` `_TimeSlotScreenState`)

### Upcoming bookings
A booking for a later slot stays **Booked** (badge, Home section "Upcoming") instead of running ahead to
captain / wash / "Check photos". Its screen shows the booking, **only the customer's own photos**, and cancel.
The demo lifecycle waits for the slot.
(`Order.slotAhead`; `store.dart` `_startLifecycle`; `live.dart` `_booked`; `common.dart` `StatusBadge(slotAhead:)`;
`auth_pay.dart` booked banner)

### Cancelling (rule change)
- Available until the wash starts, from the Home card, the Bookings list and the order screen.
- **No cash refund.** Within **15 minutes** of booking → **1 free wash**. After that → **50% of the booking's
  value as credit** (rupees) for the next booking.
- The sheet states the outcome in one line before confirming; cards only say "Cancel booking".
- After: toast, notification, a "Booking cancelled" screen, Home banner for the free wash / credit; order moves
  to Past and is excluded from "Book again".

Code: `BusinessRules.cancelWindow`, `lateCancelCreditPercent`, `lateCancelCredit()`; `Order.canCancel()`,
`inFreeCancelWindow()`, `freeWashGiven`, `creditGiven`, `creditUsed`, `value`; `Customer.credit`;
`AppStore.cancel()`; `CancelLink`, `openCancelSheet`, `CancelSheet` (moved from `live.dart` to `common.dart`).

### Checkout (C13)
- **Free wash:** shown only when the customer has one, as a green card with a switch (on by default); total ₹0;
  button "Book with free wash", skipping the payment sheet.
- **Credit:** shown only when the customer has credit ("₹75 credit · ₹75 off. You pay ₹74."), on by default;
  the total and the held amount use the reduced price; if the credit covers it all, "Book with credit".
  Not combined with a free wash. Used credit is deducted on successful booking (`Order.creditUsed`).
- Payment sheet content is scrollable as a fallback on very short screens.

(`BookingDraft.basePrice`, `creditApplied`, `price`; `screens/booking.dart` `ReviewScreen`, `PayMethodSheet`)

### Small fixes
"1 free washes" → "1 free wash" (Home, Profile).

## 3b. Captain app (evening of 24 Oct)

- **Welcome (W00):** same `PageView` carousel and tracking dots as the Customer welcome; the dots widget now lives in
  `washkrew_ui` as `CarouselDots` and both apps use it.
- **Owner cancels:** job stays in Today's plan struck through ("Cancelled by owner", or "Cancelled · trip paid" when
  the captain had set off, who then gets the trip fee); the next job becomes current; short notification only.
  (`CaptainStore.ownerCancels`, `Job.cancelled`, `Job.tripPaid`, `home.dart` `_cancelledCard`)
- **Several windows / any time:** cards show the owner's windows (`Job.windows`, `whenLabel`); any-time jobs are fitted
  next to the captain's other jobs in the same building and tagged **Flexible** (`Job.flexible`, `fitFlexible`, `_FlexTag`).
- **When I work:** Where I work has 6–10 AM / 10 AM–2 PM / 2–6 PM chips that only filter offers (`workTimes`, `wantsJob`).
- **Later-day jobs:** kept off Home; listed under **Coming up** in the Jobs tab until their day starts
  (`Job.day`, `todayJobs`, `laterJobs`).
- **Typed addresses:** **New address** tag with pincode and **Get directions** (`Job.typedAddress`, `Device.openMapsSearch`).
- **Plates:** `Fmt.plate` tidies plates for display everywhere (`WPlate`) and the Customer app saves them that way.
- Free washes and customer credit are not shown to captains; job pay is unchanged.
