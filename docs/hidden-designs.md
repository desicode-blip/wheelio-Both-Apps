# Hidden designs (kept, not deleted)

These screens are fully built and still in the code. They are switched off with flags in
`apps/ios/Customer/App/DesignFlags.swift`. Set a flag to `true` to bring the design back exactly as it was.

| Flag | What it shows | Hidden since |
|---|---|---|
| `firstWashHome` | Customer Home for first-time users: "Hi there / Your wheels are waiting", the "Book your first wash" card (car from ₹149, bike ₹69) and "How it works" (3 steps). While hidden, first-time customers land straight on "What are we washing?". | 16 Oct 2026 |
| `firstStepProgress` | "Step 1 of 5 · Vehicle" progress bar on the first booking step. Later steps still show it. | 16 Oct 2026 |

To preview the hidden Home without changing code: Screens gallery → **C40h**.
