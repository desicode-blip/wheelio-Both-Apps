Rules every screen follows

Rule
One primary action per screen. Green only to pay, approve or accept. Red only for errors and destructive actions.
One small decision per screen in the booking flow (vehicle → plate → photos → place → floor → notes → time → pay).
Money copy on every payment screen: held safely, released only after approval. Washer sees pay before accepting.
Call/help sits bottom-left on task screens. Calls between customer and washer go through masked numbers.
Photos are in-app camera only, time and location stamped. The washer’s before and after photos are the wash evidence.
Colour is never the only signal: every status is icon + word. Touch targets 48 px or more (washer 64 px).
