# WashKrew support

For the WashKrew Customer and WashKrew Captain apps, email info@washkrew.com or call +91 80885 16875. The owner must verify that the mailbox and phone are monitored before this page is published. No support-hours or response-time guarantee has been supplied.

You can also use Help/Report a problem inside the app. Include the app name, booking reference if relevant, device model and a description of the issue. Do not send OTPs, passwords, UPI PINs, full card details or identity documents by ordinary email.

To request account deletion without the app, open the account-deletion page and follow its instructions. Tell support whether the request concerns Customer or Captain. When one phone is registered in both apps, deletion applies only to the role you request.
