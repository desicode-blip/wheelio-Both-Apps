What we wash

Cars

  • Dry wash (dusting)
  • Wet wash, outside

Bikes and scooters

  • Dusting
  • Wet wash

Exterior washes by hand, with water and microfibre cloths. Inside cleaning comes later. You see the price for your vehicle before you book.

Every wash leaves a record.

Each step shows up in the app as it happens, so you never have to wonder whether the job was done. Your payment is held, and released to the captain only after you approve.

Request a wash

  1. You book

    Booked

    Pick your vehicle, the wash and a time that suits you. The price is shown before you pay.

  2. A captain accepts

    A trained captain working near you takes the job, and you can see who is coming.

  3. Photos before

    In progress

    At your parking spot, the captain photographs your vehicle before starting.

  4. The wash, then photos after

    Cleaned by hand, then photographed again so you can see the result.

  5. You approve

    Done

    Check the photos and approve the job. That is when the payment is released.

Look for the black polo and the blue cloth.

WashKrew captains arrive in uniform, with their own kit and clean microfibre cloths. You will know who is working on your vehicle, and so will your society.
